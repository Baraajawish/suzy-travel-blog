Olá, I'm Suzy

28 · Lisbon · always packing

I grew up in Lisbon watching ships leave the Tagus and wondering where they were going. Two years ago I quit my office job, bought a one-way ticket to Bangkok and started writing about everything I tasted, saw and got wrong along the way.

This blog is my travel diary: honest stories, real costs and the little places I fall in love with across Europe and Southeast Asia. If my map makes you want to pack a bag, it's doing its job.
